AI Voice & UX Framework

Aligning voice across product, support, and AI

The Problem

We were growing quickly across product, support, and AI, but our content was inconsistent and fragmented.

Without a shared voice, we risked confusing users, diluting our brand, and increasing support costs at scale.

What I Did

I partnered with product, design, support, and marketing to audit existing content, define a tone of voice grounded in our values, and build a UX writing framework covering voice, grammar, naming, and structural patterns.

I aligned AI-generated copy with our tone through testing and real prompts, and embedded the framework into design tools and day-to-day workflows as a self-serve Notion guide.

Key Findings

📖

Examples beat rules

Teams adopted the framework faster when it showed real do and don't scenarios rather than abstract guidance.

✍️

Consistency builds confidence

Support teams wrote faster and clearer replies. Designers felt more confident tackling microcopy.

🤖

AI needs voice guidance too

Without structure, AI copy drifted off-brand. With clear prompts and tone principles, it became a consistent extension of our voice.

⚖️

Structure without bloat

Teams needed clarity, not a rulebook. Getting that balance right was the hardest part of the design process.

Key insight

"Without structure, AI copy drifted off-brand. With clear tone principles, it became a consistent extension of our voice."
